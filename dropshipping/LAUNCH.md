# Launch plan

Budget: 15 KD. No paid advertising. Product: oversized wearable blanket
hoodie, sold at 89.99 USD or roughly 28 KD.

## The only guarantee available

You cannot be guaranteed a win. You can be guaranteed a small loss.

| | |
|---|---|
| Sample, the only thing you buy | 8.79 KD |
| Cash still in your pocket | 6.21 KD |
| Worst case if nobody ever buys | lose 8.79 KD, keep the blanket |
| Sales needed to break even | **one** |

Nothing else can take money from you. There is no ad spend burning while
you wait, and no inventory, because the supplier ships each order only
after a customer has paid.

One sale and you are even. Everything after that is profit.

That is the real shape of this. Not a guaranteed win, a capped loss.

## Sunday

Four things. Two or three hours.

1. **Order the sample.** One blanket, to your own address. Pick the
   supplier with the most orders and a rating above 4.6, not the cheapest.
   It arrives in about two weeks.
2. **Open the account.** One Instagram or TikTok account for the product.
   Arabic, aimed at Kuwait. Not a general store, one product.
3. **Write the price on paper.** 28 KD. Decide it now, before anyone asks,
   so you never negotiate against yourself mid-conversation.
4. **Watch fifteen competitor videos.** Note which opening second made you
   keep watching. That is the only part that matters.

Do not build a website. Do not design a logo. Do not buy a domain. None of
those have ever sold anything.

## While the sample ships

Two weeks of nothing to film. Use them.

- Write thirty video ideas. One line each.
- Learn how the supplier's returns work, in writing, before a customer asks.
- Decide how a customer pays you. In Kuwait, a bank transfer or a payment
  link is normal and costs you nothing to set up.

## Once it arrives

Post four times a week. Same product, different angles. Film yourself
wearing it, in the cold aisle of a supermarket, on the sofa, in the car.

The first second decides everything. People scroll before your sentence
finishes.

## When to stop

Decide this now, while it costs you nothing to be honest.

**Stop if, after 48 videos over twelve weeks, no video has passed 5,000
views.** That is the signal that the content is not landing, and more of
the same will not fix it.

You will have lost 8.79 KD and learned to film. That is a cheap education.

**Keep going if one video passes 20,000 views**, even if it sold nothing.
Reach is the hard part. Turning reach into sales is the easy part.

## What comes after

| Money in hand | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| 40 KD | A second product to test |
| 100 KD | Better filming, faster shipping option |
| 300 KD | Enough to test paid ads properly |

Paid advertising needs about 300 KD to answer the question "does this
product work" honestly. Below that you are guessing with real money. This
is how you climb to that number without borrowing it.
